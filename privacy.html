Puzzlot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Puzzlot ("the app") is a daily puzzle game for Android and iOS. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. Puzzlot is made by HTTGameStudio.

No accounts, no personal data stored by us

Puzzlot has no sign-up or login. Your progress (streaks, results, saved hints and puzzles) is stored only on your device. We do not run servers that collect or store information about you. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch (for a hint, a bonus puzzle or to solve a puzzle). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, only if you allow tracking on iOS),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was shown or completed).

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy options in the app.
  • iOS: the app asks for permission to track. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • If you decline ad consent, hints, bonus puzzles and solving stay available for free.

Sharing results

When you tap Share, the app hands a short text summary (puzzle number, moves, stars, streak) to your device's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

Children

Puzzlot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

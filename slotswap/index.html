Slot Swap

Slot Swap: Number Sum Puzzle, by HTTGameStudio

Place the number tiles so every row and column hits its target sum. 2000 levels that grow from small 3×3 boards to tricky 6×6 grids with decoy tiles. Every level has exactly one solution.

Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Email httgamestudio@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and the level number.

FAQ

I changed phones. Is my progress saved? Progress is stored only on the device, so a new phone starts fresh.

Do I have to watch ads? No. Videos are optional and only shown when you tap for an extra hint or to solve a level. You get a free hint every day.

How do I change my ad choices? Settings > Privacy settings.

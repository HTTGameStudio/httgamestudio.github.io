Slot Swap Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Slot Swap ("the app") is a number puzzle game for Android and iOS. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. Slot Swap is made by HTTGameStudio.

No accounts, no personal data stored by us

Slot Swap has no sign-up or login. Your progress (solved levels, stars, saved hints) and your settings (such as the board colour) are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that collect or store information about you. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch (for an extra hint or to solve a level). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, only if you allow tracking on iOS),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was shown or completed).

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy settings inside the app.
  • iOS: the app asks for permission to track. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • If you decline ad consent, hints and solving stay available for free.

Sharing results

When you tap Share, the app hands a short text summary (level number, moves, hints used, stars) to your device's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

Children

Slot Swap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Wall Duel

Wall Duel: Strategy, by HTTGameStudio

Reach the other side before your rival does, and use your walls to make their path longer. A quick strategy duel on a glowing neon board: 50 levels in 5 worlds, a daily puzzle, and matches against a friend on one phone or online with a room code.

Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Email httgamestudio@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and what happened.

FAQ

I changed phones. Is my progress saved? Progress is stored only on the device, so a new phone starts fresh.

I bought No Ads or the Starter Pack. How do I get it back? Open Settings > Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Google or Apple account you bought it with.

Do I have to watch ads? Reward ads are always optional. Short break ads can appear between matches; "No Ads" removes them.

How do I play online? One player taps Online and creates a room, the other joins with the room code. Both phones need an internet connection.

How do I change my ad choices? Settings > Privacy Options.

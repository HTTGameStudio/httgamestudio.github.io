Wall Duel Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

Wall Duel ("the game", listed in the stores as Wall Duel: Strategy) is a strategy game for Android, iPhone and iPad, made by HTTGameStudio (United Kingdom). This policy explains what data the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

1. Data we do not collect

We do not run our own servers and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. There are no accounts, no chat and no user profiles in the game.

2. Data stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, items, settings, quest progress) and the year of birth you enter on first launch are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them. Your phone's own backup (Google or iCloud) may copy app data if you have turned backup on.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional reward ads that you choose to watch, and occasional break ads between matches. Players who buy "No Ads" or the Starter Pack do not see break ads.

To show and measure ads, Google may process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple IDFA only if you allow tracking),
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was shown, completed or tapped),
  • diagnostics about the ad software (crashes and performance).
  • UK, EEA and Switzerland: before any personalised ads, the game shows Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy Options.
  • iPhone and iPad: the game may ask for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, or you are under 16, the advertising identifier is not shared.
  • Players under 16: on first launch the game asks for a year of birth. If you are under 16, you only get non-personalised ads with content suitable for children, and the consent form is not used for you.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings, or turn off tracking in your iPhone settings.

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details. The game receives a confirmation of what was bought (product and transaction id) so it can unlock it on your device.

5. Online play (Unity Gaming Services)

When you create or join an online room, the game signs in anonymously to Unity Gaming Services. Unity processes an anonymous player id, the room code and the moves of the match so both phones can play. Rooms are private, last only as long as the match, and contain no names or messages. Online play is optional.

Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

6. Children

Wall Duel is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because younger players may still play, the game applies the protections in section 3 to anyone who says they are under 16.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR or KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to processing. Because we hold no personal data on our own systems, most requests are handled by uninstalling the game, using Settings > Privacy Options, or contacting Google or Unity directly. You can also write to us and we will help.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

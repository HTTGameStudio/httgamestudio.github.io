Puzzlot

A new dial-lock logic puzzle every day. Turn the dials until every column hits its target sum, in as few moves as you can. Everyone gets the same lock, so you can compare results with friends.

FAQ

When does the daily puzzle change? At midnight, your local time.

I changed phones. Is my streak saved? Progress is stored only on the device, so a new phone starts fresh.

Can I play without ads? Yes. Videos are optional: you only see one if you choose a hint, a bonus puzzle or to solve a puzzle.
